# 2026.10.28-emf-mapping-clip-candidate

The partial EMF inspector now tracks anisotropic window and viewport extent
scaling, five predefined physical mapping modes using the reference device in
the EMF header, and ignored extent settings in fixed mapping modes. Extent
scaling truncation and no-op cases are tested; overflow is marked unknown.

Intersect and exclude rectangles are recorded separately as device-space
constraints, including clipping offsets, SaveDC/RestoreDC and null-copy reset.
Unknown metaclip and unknown mappings remain unknown. Truncated records and
non-finite transforms are rejected. The inspector does not trim geometry or
simulate exact device-region rounding. MM_ISOTROPIC, arbitrary region combines,
clip paths, complete bitmap and rendering semantics remain unsupported.

On Windows, the full direct-PDF suite reported 1,005 passed, 7 subtests passed,
and 3 failures caused by missing symbolic-link privilege (WinError 1314),
before the final small boundary checks. After those changes, 108 related tests
passed. Synthetic mappings and clipping were compared with native Windows GDI.
The no-excuse Python checker reported no violations in 19 figure-audit files;
basedpyright and ruff were not run. Six local EMFs were inspected as partial
extracts, not used to claim source fidelity or production approval.

This is a release candidate, not a runtime deployment, full EMF renderer,
InDesign placement validation or PDF/X certification.
