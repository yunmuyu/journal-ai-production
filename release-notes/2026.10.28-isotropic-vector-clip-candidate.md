# 2026.10.28-isotropic-vector-clip-candidate

EMF MM_ISOTROPIC accepts explicitly supplied playback DPI and models GDI
viewport adjustment, including extent scaling. Synthetic cases were compared
to Windows GDI on a device with equal horizontal and vertical DPI; other device
characteristics remain to be checked. Playback DPI is never inferred from the
EMF reference-device header.

Optional vector clipping models rectangle-region COPY, AND, OR, XOR and DIFF,
meta clipping, offsets and SaveDC/RestoreDC. It emits clipped stroke centerlines
and filled polygon geometry while preserving raw source geometry. A verified
playback device surface must be supplied; EMF ink bounds are not the surface.
The optional `requirements-clip.txt` pins Shapely 2.1.2 (BSD-3-Clause, bundled
GEOS LGPL-2.1). User-controlled dependencies remain outside the runtime.

Ellipses, text glyph outlines, clip paths, bitmap graphics, complete stroke
outlines, GDI pixel-region rounding and general EMF rendering are not covered.
Unknown states and unsupported objects remain explicit; extraction is partial.

Windows full direct-PDF regression reported 1,029 passed, 7 subtests passed and
3 failures caused by missing symbolic-link privilege (WinError 1314), before
the final supplementary tests and small checks. After those changes, 97
focused EMF and crop tests passed, and the Python no-excuse checker reported no
violations in 20 inspection modules. Native Windows GDI was used as an oracle
for isotropic mapping, five region combinations, metaclip and sheared clipping.
Basedpyright and ruff were not run. No production runtime deployment, Adobe
placement, PDF/X certification or complete-source fidelity is claimed.
